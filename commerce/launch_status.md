## Merchant activation requirement

The Shopify store is still unclaimed. Shopify will not accept live customer payments or release automatic post-payment delivery until the store owner completes its merchant ownership and payment-provider activation. This is a platform identity and banking requirement; it cannot be performed by an automation agent or bypassed safely. Until it is activated, the storefront can create a checkout session but a customer cannot complete a paid order.
