# FlipProfit Deal Screening Bundle

## Product

**FlipProfit Deal Screening Bundle** is a one-time digital download for independent vehicle and equipment resellers who want a visible record of the numbers behind a possible purchase.

## Launch price

**$19 USD, one time.**

## Buyer receives

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `FlipProfit_Deal_Screening_Workbook.xlsx` | A blank Excel workbook with a work order, visible calculation trace, local ledger, and evidence notes. |
| `FlipProfit_Field_Checklist.pdf` | A two-page printable checklist for buy, repair, transport, paperwork, and listing preparation. |

## Customer-facing description

> **Know your walk-away price before the gate opens.**
>
> FlipProfit gives you a blank, traceable work order for the costs around a vehicle or equipment flip: buy price, fees, transport, repairs, title, detail, selling costs, and your required profit. It does not invent a sale price or promise a profit. You enter the facts you have, then the workbook shows the math behind the deal.

## Customer-facing bullets

* Start with a blank work order—no sample deals, fake prices, or pretend results.
* Separate what you know from what still needs to be checked.
* Calculate your total investment, selling costs, net result, break-even price, and target sale price.
* Keep a simple ledger of confirmed work orders.
* Print the field checklist and record evidence during inspection, pickup, repair, and listing.

## Delivery promise

The buyer receives the ZIP file containing the Excel workbook and PDF checklist immediately after successful checkout. The ZIP is approximately 17 KB and does not require an account or a subscription to use.

## What it does not promise

The bundle does not appraise a vehicle, determine condition, predict a sale, provide legal/title advice, or guarantee a profit. A calculation is only as reliable as the buyer’s entered and verified facts.
